What Makes a Great Bourbon?

By law, bourbon must be made in the USA from a mash of at least 51% corn, aged in new charred oak containers. The best bourbons balance sweetness, spice, fruit and oak.

Top Bourbons 2025

Blanton's Original Single Barrel — $64.99

The original single barrel bourbon. Each bottle hand-selected and stamped with the iconic metal horse stopper. Rich caramel, vanilla and citrus. Buy it the moment you see it in stock.

Eagle Rare 10 Year — $37.99

One of the best-value allocated bourbons. Aged 10 years in single barrels with notes of orange peel, herbs and honey. Extraordinary complexity above its price point.

Woodford Reserve Double Oaked — $49.99

A second maturation in heavily toasted new oak delivers dark chocolate, honey and vanilla. Deeply rewarding.

Wild Turkey Rare Breed — $39.99

The best barrel-proof value on the market. Uncut and unfiltered at 58% ABV — intense and bold.

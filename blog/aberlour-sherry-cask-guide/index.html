Aberlour: Speyside's Sherry Cask Specialist

By Caskworth Editorial August 27, 20266 min readScotch Brand Spotlight

Few distilleries lean into sherry cask character as confidently as Aberlour — here's how the Double Cask range and cask-strength A'bunadh compare.

Double Cask Maturation, Explained

Aberlour's signature technique is Double Cask maturation: whisky matured separately in both American oak and European sherry-seasoned oak casks, then married together. The result is a house style built around sherry sweetness, dried fruit and warm spice — richer and rounder than whisky matured only in ex-bourbon barrels.

The Double Cask Range

Aberlour 12 Year Double Cask — $44.99

Sherry, honey and light spice make this an ideal introduction to the sherry-forward Speyside style, and one of the best value bottles in the category.

Aberlour 16 Year Double Cask — $89.99

Rich fruit, toffee and warm spice deepen noticeably here, with the extra years in cask giving the sherry influence more room to develop.

Aberlour 18 Year — $149.99

Rich sherry, Christmas cake and profound spice at 43% ABV — this is Aberlour at its most complex within the standard range, and a genuinely serious sipping whisky.

Cask Strength: A'bunadh

Aberlour A'bunadh Cask Strength — $69.99

Bottled at 61.2% ABV with no chill filtration and no age statement, A'bunadh is matured entirely in ex-Oloroso sherry casks. Intense sherry, dark fruit and rich spice come through undiluted — this is the bottle serious sherry-cask fans reach for, and it remains one of the best cask-strength values on the market.

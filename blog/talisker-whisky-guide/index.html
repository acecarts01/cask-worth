Talisker: The Whisky Made by the Sea

By Caskworth Editorial August 27, 20266 min readScotch Brand Spotlight

Made a stone's throw from the Atlantic on the Isle of Skye, Talisker tastes like the sea it was built beside — here's how to buy across its range.

Skye's Only Distillery

Founded in 1830 on the rugged west coast of the Isle of Skye, Talisker remains the island's sole distillery. Its whisky carries a signature maritime character — peppery smoke, sea spray and warming spice — that puts it in its own category, distinct from both the softer Speyside malts and the heavier peat of Islay.

The Core Range

Talisker 10 Year — $54.99

The definitive introduction: peppery peat, sea spray and warm fruit at 45.8% ABV. It's the bottle that built Talisker's reputation and remains the best value entry point.

Talisker Storm — $54.99

A no-age-statement expression built around a bigger smoke profile — brine, smoke and warming spice, named for the wild weather the distillery weathers on Skye's coast.

The Age Statements

Talisker 18 Year — $149.99

Complex smoke, sea salt and dark fruit combine into a noticeably more refined dram, widely regarded as one of the best balanced whiskies Talisker produces.

Talisker 25 Year — $499.99

Ancient peat, profound fruit and old sea salt — decades of maturation soften the smoke into something layered and contemplative rather than aggressive.

Talisker 30 Year — $999.99

Timeless smoke, ancient brine and profound fruit in the oldest expression Caskworth carries from the distillery — a serious bottle for a serious collection.

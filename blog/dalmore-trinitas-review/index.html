Dalmore Trinitas: The $195,000 Scotch That Only Three People Own

By Caskworth Editorial July 11, 20269 min readUltra Rare Scotch

In 2010, The Dalmore released Trinitas — a Scotch whisky so rare that only three bottles were ever produced. Each was sold privately for $195,000. The Trinitas remains a landmark in the brief and extraordinary history of ultra-luxury whisky.

What Is Dalmore Trinitas?

Trinitas is a blend of four exceptionally rare Dalmore single casks — distilled in 1868, 1878, 1926 and 1939 — combined with mature Dalmore aged in vintage port, Madeira and Matusalem sherry casks. The oldest component is over 140 years old. The youngest is over 80.

Why Only Three Bottles?

The quantity was constrained entirely by the available stock of the 1868 component. After careful blending, Master Distiller Richard Paterson determined that exactly three perfect bottles could be produced without compromising the integrity of the spirit. No more. No less.

The Tasting Experience

Those who have experienced Trinitas describe a multi-layered journey of extraordinary dried fruits, ancient oak, leather, Madeira sweetness, spiced orange and a palate that evolves across hours. It is, by universal consensus, the most complex whisky ever produced at the Dalmore Distillery.

The Dalmore Range at Caskworth

The Dalmore 25 Year ($999.99) and Dalmore Luminary No.3 1987 ($14,999.99) are available at Caskworth — both delivering the signature Dalmore character of dark chocolate, orange and exceptional sherry cask influence.

What Straight Rye Whiskey Means

Under the US federal standards in 27 CFR 5.143, rye whisky must be made from a fermented mash of at least 51% rye grain, distilled at no more than 160 proof and stored in charred new oak barrels. Straight rye whiskey adds a minimum of two years in those barrels. Five of the bottles in this guide are listed in our catalogue as straight rye.

The 51% is a floor, so ryes range from a modest majority of rye to a much higher share. The more rye, the more pepper, spice and dryness you taste. If you are new to the style, SLB Drinks’ video The 10 Best Rye Whiskies For The Money! is a useful companion to the list below. The Best Rye for Cocktails

The best rye for cocktails is a bold bottle at 45% ABV or higher, because it keeps its character against ice and vermouth. Nine of the 12 bottles here are at 45% or above. Practical picks:

  • High West Double Rye at $35.00. The cheapest bottle in the table and at 45% a sensible everyday mixer.
  • Jack Daniel’s Rye at $39.99 and James E. Pepper at $40.99. Both straight ryes at 45%, priced for cocktails.
  • Knob Creek Rye at $44.99. A straight rye at 45% that is easy to find a use for, from Manhattans to Old Fashioneds.
  • Angel’s Envy Rye at $79.99. At 50% with a rum barrel finish, better for sipping or a special cocktail than for everyday mixing.

If you plan to make a lot of drinks, buy one cocktail rye and one for sipping, so you are never mixing your best bottle. Our guide to the rye whiskey collection lists more.

Rye to Sip Neat

For sipping, look for a higher strength or a longer age. In this group, Angel’s Envy Rye at 50% ABV is the strongest, and Masterson’s 10 Year is the only bottle with an age statement, though at 40% and as a Canadian rye it is a gentler, different drink. The 45% straight ryes also drink well neat, especially with a few drops of water.

Pour a small measure into a tulip glass, let it sit for a minute and take a first sip neat. Then add a few drops of water and notice how the aroma opens. If you like a drier, spicier whiskey, this is where rye shows what bourbon cannot.

Craft Rye and Premium American Rye

Three bottles in the table are listed as craft rye in our catalogue: Catoctin Creek Roundstone Rye (Virginia), FEW Rye Whiskey (Kentucky) and Journeyman Ravenswood Rye (Michigan). This craft rye whiskey guide uses the term as our catalogue does, and craft is a producer description, not a legal category, so read each producer’s own page for how the whiskey is made.

What craft rye offers is variety: different regions, different grain choices and smaller batches. If you already know the big names, these are a cheap way to explore. For a premium American rye above this price band, WhistlePig 10 Year at $79.99 and Sazerac 18 Year at $299.99 are covered in the showdown guide, and you can buy WhistlePig rye and buy Sazerac 18 Year on the product pages. For barrel proof rye online, check the product page for the strength, because none of the 12 bottles above is listed as barrel proof.

Canadian Rye Whisky Is Different

Two bottles here are Canadian: Dillon’s Small Batch Rye and Masterson’s 10 Year. Canadian rye whisky follows Canadian rules, not the US standard above, so do not assume a Canadian rye has the same 51% rye rule as a US straight rye. Both are bottled at 40% in our catalogue, which makes them lighter than the 45% American ryes, so judge them as a separate style. If you enjoy lighter, smoother whiskies, they are worth trying alongside an American rye, because the contrast shows quickly and clearly how much the rules shape the glass in front of you. Masterson’s adds a 10 year age statement at $86.99, which makes it the best choice here for anyone who values age over strength, while Dillon’s at $49.95 is the easier first step into Canadian rye.

How to Choose

A simple way to pick:

  1. Start with the use. Cocktails need strength, so choose 45% or higher. Sipping can handle either.
  2. Pick a budget. Under $40, High West or Jack Daniel’s. Around $45, Knob Creek, Dad’s Hat or FEW. Above $75, Angel’s Envy or Masterson’s.
  3. Choose your region. Kentucky, Tennessee, Pennsylvania, Virginia and Michigan are all in the list, so you can compare.
  4. Buy two bottles from different groups, then taste them side by side.

A Three-Bottle Starter Set

If you are new to rye, a three-bottle set gives you a cocktail rye, a straight rye and a craft rye for $125.98: High West Double Rye ($35.00), Knob Creek Rye ($44.99) and FEW Rye Whiskey ($45.99). Taste them side by side, neat first and then with a few drops of water. Note which you find spicier, which is sweeter and which has the longest finish.

Then use what you learn. If you prefer the softer one, look for ryes at 45% with more sweetness. If you prefer the spicier one, move toward higher strengths such as Angel’s Envy at 50%. Three bottles is enough to answer the question of what you like without a large outlay, and it gives you a base for cocktails at home, whether you favour the Manhattan, the Old Fashioned or a simple rye and ginger ale. Common mistakes to avoid are buying on the label alone, assuming a higher price means a better fit and skipping the strength, which changes how a rye mixes.

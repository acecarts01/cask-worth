Hibiki Harmony: Why It Is Japan's Most Award-Winning Whisky

By Caskworth Editorial July 11, 20267 min readJapanese Whisky

Hibiki Harmony is the most decorated Japanese blended whisky in the world.

What Is Hibiki Harmony?

Created by Suntory using malt whiskies from Yamazaki and Hakushu distilleries plus grain whisky from Chita. Matured in five cask types — American white oak, sherry, Mizunara oak, bourbon and plum liqueur casks.

Tasting Notes

Honey, white chocolate and orange blossom on the nose. Silky smooth with rose, lychee and subtle sandalwood. The finish is long and harmonious.

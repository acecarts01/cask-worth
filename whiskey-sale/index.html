What are the best Christmas whiskey cocktails and drinks?

The simplest Christmas whiskey cocktail is the Old Fashioned. The International Bartenders Association recipe uses 45 ml of bourbon or rye, one sugar cube, two dashes of Angostura bitters and a few drops of water, stirred over ice and finished with an orange peel. It suits almost any bourbon or rye in the sale.

For whiskey christmas drinks that need little effort, pour a good bourbon over ice, add a twist of orange, and serve. Many people reach for Jack Daniel’s Christmas whiskey ideas, such as the Jack Daniel's Old No. 7, because it is easy to find and easy to mix. If you are hosting, a set of Christmas whiskey glasses makes the pour feel special, which is why the Woodford Reserve Gift Set with Glasses is a popular choice.
